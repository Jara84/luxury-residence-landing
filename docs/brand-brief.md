# Brand Brief
## Residencia El Cerro

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved

---

# 1. Project Vision

Create a premium digital experience that allows visitors to emotionally connect with the residence before scheduling a visit.

The website should feel timeless, elegant and exclusive.

The objective is not simply to show an apartment.

The objective is to sell a lifestyle.

---

# 2. Target Audience

Primary Audience

• Executives
• Entrepreneurs
• Investors
• International buyers
• Families looking for a premium residence in Medellín

Age

35–65 years

Income

Upper-middle to high income.

People willing to pay for quality, design, location and comfort.

---

# 3. Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

• Elegant
• Contemporary
• Sophisticated
• Warm
• Quiet
• Exclusive

The brand should NEVER feel:

• Aggressive
• Cheap
• Overloaded
• Flashy
• Corporate

---

# 4. Visual Principles

Less is more.

Large photography.

Generous white space.

Strong typography.

Few but meaningful animations.

Every section must breathe.

---

# 5. Color Palette

Primary

Stone
White
Charcoal

Accent

Warm gold (used sparingly)

Support

Soft gray

No saturated colors.

---

# 6. Typography

Primary font

Manrope

Headings

Large
Elegant
Minimal

Body

Comfortable reading
High contrast

---

# 7. Photography Style

Photography is the hero.

Images should communicate:

Natural light

Space

Premium finishes

Warmth

Views

Avoid excessive overlays.

Let the photography speak.

---

# 8. User Experience

The navigation should feel effortless.

Every section must answer one question:

"Why would I want to live here?"

Scrolling should feel cinematic.

---

# 9. Copywriting Style

Short paragraphs.

Powerful headlines.

Few words.

Every sentence must create desire.

Do not describe.

Inspire.

---

# 10. Development Principles

Reusable components.

Consistent spacing.

Consistent typography.

Mobile first.

Accessibility.

Performance before visual effects.

---

# 11. Success Criteria

A visitor should finish the landing thinking:

"I want to schedule a visit."

Not:

"I finished reading a website."

---

# Design North Star

Luxury through simplicity.
